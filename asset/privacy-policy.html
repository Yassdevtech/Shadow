Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ShadowSoft ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the AirPlay - Screen Mirroring mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App. If you contact us for customer support, we may collect your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and device settings relevant to app functionality.
  • Network Information: Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and local IP addresses — used solely for discovering Smart TVs and media devices on your local network for screen mirroring and media casting functionality.
  • Usage Data: General app usage statistics, crash reports, and performance data to help us improve the App.
  • Advertising Data: Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) used by our advertising partners to serve relevant ads. This is not collected for Premium subscribers who have opted out of ads.

1.3 Subscription Information

If you subscribe to our Premium plan, all payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details, bank account information, or any other financial data. We only receive a confirmation of your subscription status (active or expired) from Google Play to enable Premium features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Purpose Data Used
Device discovery (Smart TV scanning) Wi-Fi network info, local IP addresses
Screen mirroring and media casting Device info, network info
Serving advertisements (Basic plan) Advertising ID, device info
App improvement and bug fixing Crash reports, usage data
Managing your subscription Subscription status from Google Play
Customer support Email address (if you contact us)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements to users on the Basic (free) plan. Subscription purchases are processed through Google Play. Google manages all payment data. We do not have access to your payment information.
