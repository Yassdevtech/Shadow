Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Shadow Soft ("Shadow Studio", "we", "us") publishes the mobile apps SmartLink: Cast to TV & Mirror, Endoscope Camera / USB OTG, Smart Remote: Universal TV and Wireless Charging Checker (the "Apps"), and the website shadowsoft.space. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use them, how it is used, and the choices you have.

BY INSTALLING OR USING OUR APPS, YOU AGREE TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE, PLEASE DO NOT INSTALL OR USE THEM.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our apps do not require an account or registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or payment card details, and we do not sell your personal data.

We do not collect, upload or store your photos, videos or recordings on our servers. For example, photos and videos captured with Endoscope Camera / USB OTG stay on your device, and content you cast to a TV is streamed directly from your phone.

Information Processed on Your Device

To work properly, our apps may use certain device features. This processing happens on your device and you can control it through your Android permissions:

  • Local network / WiFi: to discover and connect to your TV or streaming device (SmartLink, Smart Remote). Android may require the location permission to discover nearby WiFi devices; we use it only to find your devices.
  • USB / camera access: to connect to your endoscope or USB camera (Endoscope Camera / USB OTG).
  • Storage and media access: to show your gallery, cast your own content or save captured photos and videos.
  • Battery information: level, temperature, voltage, health and charging state, to display charging features and alerts (Wireless Charging Checker).

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Our apps are free and contain ads. To show ads and understand how the apps are used, we integrate third-party services:

  • AdMob by Google: for advertisement serving.
  • Google Analytics: for monitoring app usage and analyzing user behavior.

These services may automatically collect information such as your device or advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, app interactions, ad interactions and approximate location. They collect and use this information under their own privacy policies, and may combine it with information from other services. As declared on Google Play, our apps may share device or other IDs and location data with these third parties. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in apps.

How We Use Information

The information described above is used to:

  • provide and operate the features of our apps;
  • serve and measure advertising;
  • analyze usage and improve the stability and quality of our apps;
  • provide customer and technical support when you contact us;
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be disclosed only:

  • to service providers, such as advertising and analytics partners, that process information as described in this policy;
  • when required by law, or by law enforcement or government authorities in connection with legal obligations or investigations;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, our apps or others.

Your Choices and Rights

You are in control of your information:

  • Ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Permissions: you can review or revoke any app permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Uninstall: you can stop all data collection by our apps by uninstalling them.
  • Requests: you can ask us about, or request access to, correction or deletion of any personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law (including Canadian privacy laws and, where applicable, the GDPR), by contacting us below.

Data Retention and Security

We do not operate user accounts or personal profiles. Information collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If an app offers a subscription or in-app purchase, the payment is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. See each app's Terms of Use for details.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with COPPA. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our apps after changes means you accept the updated policy.
